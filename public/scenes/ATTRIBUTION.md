# Credits for four source-based panoramas

This notice applies only to the four panoramas listed below and their `-thumb.webp` previews. Each flat source photograph was expanded into a spherical illustration; the output can change photographed details as well as invent the unseen surroundings. Real-ESRGAN x4plus enlarged 1774 × 887 generated images to 7096 × 3548; that is not native archival resolution or new historical evidence. Originals are retained in the photograph album. Each derivative is distributed under the corresponding source ShareAlike license.
